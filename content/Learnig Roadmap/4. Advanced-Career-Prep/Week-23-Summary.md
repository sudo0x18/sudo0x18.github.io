# Week 23: APT Reports Reading & Threat Intelligence Correlation

**Objective:** Learn to read advanced threat reports and correlate with your findings.

---

### Day 1: Introduction to Threat Reports  
- Structure: Overview, TTPs, IOCs, attribution.

### Day 2: Dissecting a Mandiant/Unit42 Report  
- Extract and map indicators, malware families.

### Day 3: Mapping to MITRE ATT&CK  
- TTPs, kill chain, technique IDs.

### Day 4: Reconstructing Malware Timeline  
- Attack chain, infection vector, payload delivery.

### Day 5: Open Source TI Tools  
- ThreatConnect, MISP, ATT&CK Navigator.

### Day 6-7: Report-to-Practice Exercise  
- Pick a report → build a small dataset → analyze & map.
