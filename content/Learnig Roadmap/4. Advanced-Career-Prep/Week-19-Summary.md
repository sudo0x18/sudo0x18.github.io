# Week 19: Malware Campaign Tracking & Attribution Basics

**Objective:** Learn how to analyze and track malware campaigns, actors, and infrastructure.

---

### Day 1: Understanding Campaigns & TTPs  
- What are malware campaigns?  
- Introduction to MITRE ATT&CK and TTP-based attribution.

### Day 2: Introduction to Threat Intelligence Feeds  
- Using OSINT sources (VirusTotal, Any.run, URLhaus, etc.)

### Day 3: Tracking IOCs Across Campaigns  
- Hashes, domains, IPs, mutexes.  
- Passive DNS analysis.

### Day 4: Malware Families Overview  
- Research about Emotet, Trickbot, Cobalt Strike.

### Day 5: Group Attribution 101  
- How security researchers attribute malware to APT groups.

### Day 6-7: Practice Tracking Campaigns  
- Select 1 malware family, analyze recent campaigns, and document findings.
